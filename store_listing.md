## Full Description (≤4000 chars)

```
🔥 Build lasting habits. Track your streaks. Journal your journey.

Loop is the all-in-one habit tracker designed to help you build a better routine — one day at a time. Whether you're trying to read more, meditate daily, or hit the gym, Loop keeps you consistent with streaks, smart reminders, and beautiful progress charts.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✨ WHY LOOP?

Unlike other habit trackers that overwhelm you with features, Loop keeps things simple and motivating. Check off habits, watch your streaks grow, and see real progress — all in a gorgeous dark-mode interface.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📱 KEY FEATURES

◉ Habit Tracking — Create unlimited habits with custom emojis, colors, and categories. Track daily, weekly, or custom schedules.

◉ Streak Counter — Stay motivated with a live streak counter. Don't break the chain!

◉ Focus Timer — Built-in Pomodoro timer with focus, short break, and long break modes. Customize durations and auto-start options.

◉ Journal & Diary — Reflect on your day with multiple diary templates. Write entries, track moods, and build a journaling habit.

◉ Analytics Dashboard — Beautiful charts showing your completion rates, weekly trends, and monthly progress at a glance.

◉ Gamification — Earn XP, level up, and unlock achievements as you build consistency. Makes habit building fun!

◉ Smart Reminders — Set custom reminder times for each habit so you never forget.

◉ Cloud Backup — Sign in with Google to back up your data to Google Drive. Never lose your progress.

◉ Dark Mode — Stunning dark theme that's easy on the eyes and looks premium.

◉ Motivational Quotes — Daily quotes on your home screen to keep you inspired.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🎯 PERFECT FOR

• Building a morning routine
• Tracking fitness & workout habits
• Meditation & mindfulness practice
• Reading challenges
• Drinking water reminders
• Study & productivity tracking
• Any daily routine you want to stick to

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔒 YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL

Loop stores all your data locally on your device. Optional Google Drive backup gives you control over when and how your data is synced. We never sell your data.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Download Loop today and start building the habits that will change your life. 🚀
```
